Klavar

Turns piano sheet music into Klavarskribo: a vertical notation where the staff is the keyboard and time runs down the page.

Open a MusicXML file Drop it here or click to browse
MusicXML (.musicxml, .xml, .mxl) or MuseScore (.mscz)
Edit notes

Click an empty spot to add a note, or drag a note to move it. With a note selected: ↑ ↓ change pitch (Shift for an octave), ← → move in time, [ and ] change length, H switches hand, Delete removes it. Ctrl+Z undoes.

No note selected.
Layout
Black key (sits on a line) White key (sits between lines) Right hand, stems point right Left hand, stems point left

Have a PDF or a photo of sheet music? Run it through MuseScore's PDF import or Audiveris first, then export as MusicXML and open that here.

Solid line triplets mark F♯ G♯ A♯, dashed pairs mark C♯ D♯. Horizontal rules are bar lines.
